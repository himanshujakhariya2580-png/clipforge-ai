i Privacy & free usage

Video processing is intended to happen on your device. The AI speech model and FFmpeg engine download from public CDNs; browser cache may retain them. The model and browser video engine are open-source; performance depends on your browser/device. WebGPU acceleration is not guaranteed in every Safari version.

Important: automatic moment selection is a first-pass transcript-density heuristic, not a human-level viral-content guarantee. The headline is generated from the opening transcript words unless you enter one manually. Face tracking, true smart reframing, and silence-aware jump cuts are not included in this prototype yet.
